# Agile Lab — Team Guidelines (demo document)

## Team structure
Each team has 3–4 members. One member acts as Product Owner, one as Scrum Master, and the rest form the Development Team. Roles may rotate after Sprint 2.

## Sprints
- Four sprints of two weeks each.
- Sprint planning on the first lab day of every sprint: select stories from the product backlog, agree a sprint goal and estimate story points with planning poker.
- Daily stand-up (asynchronous on the team channel on non-lab days): what I did, what I will do, blockers.
- Sprint review: a live demo of the increment to the faculty guide.
- Sprint retrospective: what went well, what to improve, action items.

## Definition of Done
A story is done when the code is reviewed by a teammate, tests pass, the feature is demonstrated, documentation is updated and no critical bug is open.

## Assessment
Product backlog quality 15%, sprint execution 35%, final product 30%, report and viva 20%.
