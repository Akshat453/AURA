Computer Lab Safety and Usage SOP (demo document)

1. Students must sign the lab register on entry and exit.
2. Food and drinks are not allowed near the workstations.
3. Report damaged equipment to the lab assistant immediately; do not open cabinets or network racks.
4. Save work on the shared drive; local disks are cleaned every Friday.
5. Switch off monitors at the end of the session. The last batch switches off the air conditioning.
6. In case of fire, use the exit near Lab-3 and assemble at the parking area behind Block E.
7. Lab timings: Monday to Friday 09:00–17:00. Extra sessions need written approval from the course instructor.
